# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Social Bond website project welcomes respectful participation from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. We aim to keep project spaces constructive, inclusive, and focused on improving the software.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project discussions.
- Discuss code and ideas without attacking the people who contribute them.
- Accept good-faith feedback and make room for different viewpoints.
- Use inclusive language and respect people's identities, names, and boundaries.
- Share only information and assets you have permission to disclose.
- Keep credentials, private customer information, and personal data out of public posts and examples.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, persistent disruption, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, retaliation, or publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Deliberately sharing secrets or private customer details is also prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in the GitHub repository, its issues, pull requests, code reviews, and other spaces used to discuss or maintain this project. It also applies when someone is representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately to maintainer Brajesh Kumar through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/brajesh31). Include a link or factual account and enough context to review it. Do not put private details in a public issue. For suspected vulnerabilities, use the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer will review reports and take a proportionate response as time permits. Responses may include a request to stop, a warning, removal of content, temporary limits, or exclusion from project spaces. Privacy will be respected as far as reasonably possible. Retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
